This is a personal modification of [Antistasi Ultimate](https://github.com/Antistasi-Ultimate-Community/A3-Antistasi-Ultimate) for use by our gaming group.

The changes are some hacks+adjustments to get it to work for our missions the way we want,
mostly changing faction equipment and arms dealer stock.

Credit for the fantastic Antistasi Ultimate Mod goes to the developers of the original mod.

In a similar manner, a version of the [6 x 6 All Terrain Vehicle Mod](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1190934425) by the_lazy_seagull is also being modified for our needs here.

Both their licenses remain in place.
